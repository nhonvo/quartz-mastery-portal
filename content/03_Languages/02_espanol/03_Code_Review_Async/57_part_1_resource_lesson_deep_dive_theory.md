# 🚀 Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Master the nuances of asynchronous communication when Resolviendo Conflictos de Fusión in a Latin American or Spain engineering context.

**Impact**: Improves technical collaboration, softens criticism, and adheres to DELE C1/C2 standards for professional communication.

## ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
In Spanish tech culture (both LATAM and Spain), directness can sometimes be perceived as abrasive. It is crucial to use advanced grammatical structures to soften requests, provide mentorship, and handle complex technical reviews gracefully.

**Grammar Focus**: 
- Subjunctive (Antes de que fusiones...) and Conditional.
- **LATAM vs Spain Lexical Differences**: LATAM: 'Fusionar' o 'hacer merge'. Spain: 'Integrar' o 'mergear' (slang).

## 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison

| Level | Phrase (ES) | English (EN) | Vietnamese (VN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Junior (B1)** | Tienes conflictos. Arréglalos. | You have conflicts. Fix them. | Bạn có conflict kìa. Sửa đi. |
| **Mid (B2)** | Necesitas resolver los conflictos antes de fusionar. | You need to resolve the conflicts before merging. | Bạn cần giải quyết các xung đột trước khi merge. |
| **Senior (C1/C2)** | *Noté que hay conflictos de fusión con la rama principal (main); te pediría que realices un 'rebase' interactivo para que mantengamos el historial de commits limpio antes de integrarlo.* | *I noticed there are merge conflicts with the main branch; I'd ask that you perform an interactive rebase so that we keep the commit history clean before integrating it.* | *Tôi nhận thấy có một số xung đột merge với nhánh main; tôi muốn nhờ bạn thực hiện rebase tương tác để giữ cho lịch sử commit gọn gàng trước khi tích hợp vào.* |

## 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue (LATAM/Spain Tech Workplace)
**Reviewer (Senior)**: Hola, he revisado tu Pull Request. En general, está muy bien estructurado. Sin embargo, en cuanto a resolviendo conflictos de fusión, *Noté que hay conflictos de fusión con la rama principal (main); te pediría que realices un 'rebase' interactivo para que mantengamos el historial de commits limpio antes de integrarlo.*

**Author (Junior)**: ¡Hola! Gracias por la revisión detallada. Entiendo perfectamente. ¿Crees que esto nos tome mucho esfuerzo en el sprint actual?

**Reviewer (Senior)**: No necesariamente. Si abstraemos bien los componentes, *podríamos* lograrlo sin comprometer el pase a producción.

## 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Direct Commands**: Using imperative instead of conditional or subjunctive.
- **Literal Translation**: Using Spanglish terms indiscriminately when correct Spanish tech jargon exists.
- **Lack of Empathy**: Failing to acknowledge the effort before suggesting changes.
