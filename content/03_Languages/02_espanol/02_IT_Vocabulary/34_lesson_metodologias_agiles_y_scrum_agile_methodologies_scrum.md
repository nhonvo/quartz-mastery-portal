# 🚀 Lesson: Metodologías Ágiles y Scrum (Agile Methodologies & Scrum)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal:** Master the terminology, phrasing, and executive communication for Agile Methodologies & Scrum in Spanish engineering environments.
**Impact:** Lead technical discussions, manage Agile Methodologies & Scrum lifecycles, and communicate effectively with LATAM and Spain counterparts without sacrificing technical fidelity.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
In Spanish engineering contexts, Agile Methodologies & Scrum terminology is a blend of localized vocabulary and English loanwords. 
- **LATAM vs Spain Lexical Callouts:** In LATAM, it is common to rely on direct English equivalents (Spanglish), whereas Spain typically favors calques or direct translations (e.g., *ordenador* vs *computadora*, *despliegue* vs *deploy*).
- **Executive Engineering Depth (C1/C2):** Moving beyond basic transactional vocabulary, senior engineers must express architecture tradeoffs, systemic optimizations, and root cause analyses con estructuras gramaticales precisas (e.g., subjunctive and conditional moods).

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1):** "Necesito ayuda con el problema." (I need help with the problem.)
- **Senior (C1/C2):** "Durante la retrospectiva del sprint, debemos identificar los bloqueos en el flujo de valor y ajustar nuestra velocidad (velocity) para mejorar el burndown chart."

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue (LATAM vs Spain Tech Workplace)
**Dev (LATAM):** "En la daily, comenta que estamos bloqueados con el ticket de Jira, el sprint corre peligro."
**Lead (Spain):** "Vale, en la reunión diaria (daily) lo comento. Si no resolvemos el impedimento, no alcanzaremos el objetivo del sprint (sprint goal)."
**Bilingual VN Context (Vietnamese translation):**
* Trong buổi daily, hãy báo cáo rằng chúng ta đang bị chặn ở ticket Jira, sprint đang gặp rủi ro. / Được, trong cuộc họp hàng ngày tôi sẽ nói. Nếu không giải quyết được trở ngại, chúng ta sẽ không đạt được mục tiêu của sprint. *

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Native Mistake:** Failing to adopt the standardized industry terms across borders (LATAM vs Spain), causing friction in multicultural tech teams.
- **Non-Native Mistake:** Direct translation of idioms (e.g. using "hacer sentido" for "makes sense" instead of "tener sentido").
