# 87. PTO and Benefits (Vacaciones y Beneficios)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Negotiating Paid Time Off (PTO), health insurance, and other non-monetary benefits.

**Engineering Impact**: Equips software engineers with the exact DELE-aligned Spanish phrasing to professionally discuss total compensation, highlighting C1/C2 executive engineering depth and LATAM vs Spain lexical differences.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **DELE Grammar Focus**: Conditional tense for polite requests (*Me gustaría*, *Estaría dispuesto a*), Subjunctive for hypotheticals (*Si me ofrecieran...*).
- **Tone**: Assertive yet collaborative. LATAM favors slightly more indirect politeness (*¿Sería posible...?*) compared to Spain's directness (*Me gustaría plantear...*).
- **C1/C2 Executive Depth**: Senior engineers must frame compensation as a reflection of business value, ROI, and scalable impact, rather than just personal desire.

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
| Level | Spanish Phrasing (ES) | English (EN) | Vietnamese (VN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Junior (B1)** | Quiero más días libres. | I want more days off. | Tôi muốn nhiều ngày nghỉ hơn. |
| **Senior (C1/C2)** | Si no hay flexibilidad en el salario base, ¿estarían dispuestos a conceder 5 días adicionales de vacaciones pagadas (PTO)? | If there is no flexibility in the base salary, would you be willing to grant an additional 5 days of Paid Time Off (PTO)? | Nếu không có sự linh hoạt về lương cơ bản, bạn có sẵn lòng cấp thêm 5 ngày nghỉ phép có lương (PTO) không? |
| **LATAM Variant** | ¿Cuántos días de vacaciones de ley y días económicos ofrecen? | How many statutory vacation days and personal days do you offer? | Bạn cung cấp bao nhiêu ngày nghỉ phép theo luật định và ngày nghỉ cá nhân? |

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
**Context**: HR Recruiter (Reclutador) and Senior Backend Engineer (Candidato).
- **Reclutador**: Te hemos enviado la oferta oficial. ¿Qué te parece?
- **Candidato**: El salario base está bien, pero veo que ofrecen 15 días de vacaciones. ¿Sería posible negociar esto a 20 días? (*The base salary is fine, but I see you offer 15 vacation days. Would it be possible to negotiate this to 20 days?* / *Lương cơ bản là ổn, nhưng tôi thấy bạn cung cấp 15 ngày nghỉ phép. Liệu có thể đàm phán lên 20 ngày không?*)
- **Reclutador**: Las vacaciones están estandarizadas para todos los empleados.
- **Candidato**: Comprendo. ¿Ofrecen entonces algún seguro médico con cobertura ampliada o días adicionales por bienestar (wellness days)? (*I understand. Do you then offer health insurance with expanded coverage or additional wellness days?* / *Tôi hiểu. Vậy bạn có cung cấp bảo hiểm y tế với phạm vi bảo hiểm mở rộng hoặc thêm ngày nghỉ chăm sóc sức khỏe không?*)

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake**: Using *Yo quiero* (I want) sounds demanding.
- **Correction**: Use *Me gustaría*, *Estaría buscando* or *Me interesa alinear* (I would like / I would be looking for / I am interested in aligning).

## Part 3: Assessment Exam

### ❓ MCQs (5)
1. Which phrase is the most polite way to introduce a counteroffer?
   - [ ] A. Quiero más dinero.
   - [ ] B. Me gustaría explorar la posibilidad de...
   - [ ] C. Tienen que mejorar esto.
2. What is the C1/C2 way to mention market value?
   - [ ] A. Yo valgo más.
   - [ ] B. Mi valor de mercado es alto.
   - [ ] C. Un ajuste reflejaría mejor las condiciones actuales del mercado.
3. How do you correctly use the subjunctive to propose a hypothetical?
   - [ ] A. Si me ofrecen más, acepto.
   - [ ] B. Si me ofrecieran una revisión, estaría dispuesto a firmar hoy.
   - [ ] C. Si ustedes pagan más, yo trabajo.
4. Which term is more common in Spain for "bonus"?
   - [ ] A. Prima / Plus
   - [ ] B. Bono
   - [ ] C. Regalo
5. How to politely decline an exploding offer while asking for time?

### 🧪 Fill-in-the-Blanks (5)
