# Topic: Equity and Stock Options (Acciones y Opciones sobre Acciones)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal:** Master the terminology and negotiation strategies for equity, stock options, and RSUs in Spanish.
**Engineering Impact:** Essential for joining startups or publicly traded tech companies where equity is a major part of Total Compensation. Negotiating equity packages correctly requires high linguistic precision in formal Spanish to avoid critical contractual misunderstandings.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **Acciones:** Stocks/Shares.
- **Opciones sobre acciones (Stock Options):** The right to buy stock at a specific price.
- **Unidades de Acciones Restringidas (RSUs):** Restricted Stock Units.
- **Período de consolidación / adquisición (Vesting period):** The time you must work to earn the shares.
- **Precipicio (Cliff):** The initial period before any shares vest.
- **Evento de liquidez:** Liquidity event (e.g., IPO or acquisition).

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1):** "¿Me dan acciones de la empresa?" (Do you give me company shares?)
- **Senior (C1/C2):** "Me gustaría revisar el cronograma de consolidación de las RSUs. ¿Existe un 'cliff' de un año estándar para el otorgamiento del paquete de acciones y qué sucede en caso de un evento de liquidez?" (I'd like to review the vesting schedule of the RSUs. Is there a standard one-year cliff for the equity grant, and what happens in the event of a liquidity event?)
- **LATAM vs Spain Callouts:** In Spain, formal contracts often use "Plan de retribución en acciones" or "Stock Options" directly. In LATAM (e.g., Mexico, Colombia), you will often hear "Paquete de acciones" or "Participación accionaria". The term "vesting" is highly anglicized in startups across both regions, but formal legal documents will strictly use "consolidación".

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
*Context: Negotiating a startup offer.*
**Eng (EN/VN):** He revisado la oferta. El salario base me parece adecuado, pero me interesa saber más sobre el paquete de equidad. ¿Cuál es el precio de ejercicio de las opciones? 
*(EN: I have reviewed the offer. The base salary seems adequate, but I'm interested in knowing more about the equity package. What is the strike price of the options?)*
*(VN: Tôi đã xem xét lời đề nghị. Mức lương cơ bản có vẻ ổn, nhưng tôi muốn biết thêm về gói cổ phần. Giá thực hiện của các quyền chọn là bao nhiêu?)*

**HR (EN/VN):** El precio de ejercicio actual es de $2 USD, con un período de consolidación de 4 años y un 'cliff' de un año. 
*(EN: The current strike price is $2 USD, with a 4-year vesting period and a one-year cliff.)*
*(VN: Giá thực hiện hiện tại là 2 USD, với thời gian trao quyền là 4 năm và thời hạn chờ là một năm.)*

**Eng (EN/VN):** Entendido. ¿Qué ocurre con las acciones no consolidadas si hay un evento de liquidez o si la empresa es adquirida? ¿Existe alguna cláusula de aceleración?
*(EN: Understood. What happens to unvested shares if there is a liquidity event or if the company is acquired? Is there any acceleration clause?)*
*(VN: Đã hiểu. Điều gì xảy ra với các cổ phiếu chưa được trao quyền nếu có sự kiện thanh khoản hoặc nếu công ty bị mua lại? Có điều khoản đẩy nhanh nào không?)*

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Native:** Using English terms entirely (Vesting, Cliff) without knowing the Spanish equivalents, which might be required in formal legal contracts in Spain/LATAM.
- **Non-native:** Confusing "acciones" (actual shares) with "opciones" (the right to buy). Or using "acciones" when referring to RSUs ("unidades de acciones restringidas").
