# 82. Stock Options and RSU (Opciones sobre Acciones y RSU)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Discussing equity, vesting schedules, and Restricted Stock Units (RSUs).

**Engineering Impact**: Equips software engineers with the exact DELE-aligned Spanish phrasing to professionally discuss total compensation, highlighting C1/C2 executive engineering depth and LATAM vs Spain lexical differences.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **DELE Grammar Focus**: Conditional tense for polite requests (*Me gustaría*, *Estaría dispuesto a*), Subjunctive for hypotheticals (*Si me ofrecieran...*).
- **Tone**: Assertive yet collaborative. LATAM favors slightly more indirect politeness (*¿Sería posible...?*) compared to Spain's directness (*Me gustaría plantear...*).
- **C1/C2 Executive Depth**: Senior engineers must frame compensation as a reflection of business value, ROI, and scalable impact, rather than just personal desire.

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
| Level | Spanish Phrasing (ES) | English (EN) | Vietnamese (VN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Junior (B1)** | Quiero más acciones. | I want more shares. | Tôi muốn nhiều cổ phiếu hơn. |
| **Senior (C1/C2)** | Si el salario base es inamovible, ¿estarían dispuestos a compensar la diferencia ampliando la concesión de RSUs? | If the base salary is fixed, would you be willing to offset the difference by expanding the RSU grant? | Nếu lương cơ bản là cố định, bạn có sẵn sàng bù đắp phần chênh lệch bằng cách tăng khoản cấp RSU không? |
| **LATAM Variant** | ¿Podríamos platicar sobre el esquema de consolidación (vesting)? | Could we talk about the vesting schedule? | Chúng ta có thể nói về lịch trình trao quyền (vesting) được không? |

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
**Context**: HR Recruiter (Reclutador) and Senior Backend Engineer (Candidato).
- **Reclutador**: Te hemos enviado la oferta oficial. ¿Qué te parece?
- **Candidato**: Me interesa mucho la visión de la empresa. Para alinearme a largo plazo, me gustaría revisar el paquete de acciones. (*I'm very interested in the company's vision. To align myself long-term, I'd like to review the equity package.* / *Tôi rất quan tâm đến tầm nhìn của công ty. Để gắn bó lâu dài, tôi muốn xem xét lại gói cổ phần.*)
- **Reclutador**: Ofrecemos RSUs con un vesting de 4 años.
- **Candidato**: Comprendo. ¿Sería factible un bono inicial de retención en acciones ('cliff' ajustado)? (*I understand. Would an adjusted cliff or initial equity retention bonus be feasible?* / *Tôi hiểu. Liệu một khoản thưởng duy trì cổ phần ban đầu ('cliff' điều chỉnh) có khả thi không?*)

### 📚 Bilingual Technical Vocabulary
| Spanish (ES/LATAM) | English | Vietnamese |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Acciones Restringidas (RSU) | Restricted Stock Units | Đơn vị cổ phiếu hạn chế (RSU) |
| Período de Consolidación (Vesting) | Vesting Period | Thời gian trao quyền |
| Precipicio (Cliff) | Cliff | Thời điểm trao quyền đầu tiên (Cliff) |

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake**: Using *Yo quiero* (I want) sounds demanding.
- **Correction**: Use *Me gustaría*, *Estaría buscando* or *Me interesa alinear* (I would like / I would be looking for / I am interested in aligning).

### ❓ MCQs (5)
1. Which phrase is the most polite way to introduce a counteroffer?
   - [ ] A. Quiero más dinero.
   - [ ] B. Me gustaría explorar la posibilidad de...
   - [ ] C. Tienen que mejorar esto.
2. What is the C1/C2 way to mention market value?
   - [ ] A. Yo valgo más.
   - [ ] B. Mi valor de mercado es alto.
   - [ ] C. Un ajuste reflejaría mejor las condiciones actuales del mercado.
3. How do you correctly use the subjunctive to propose a hypothetical?
   - [ ] A. Si me ofrecen más, acepto.
   - [ ] B. Si me ofrecieran una revisión, estaría dispuesto a firmar hoy.
   - [ ] C. Si ustedes pagan más, yo trabajo.
