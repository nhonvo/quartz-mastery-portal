# 88. Handling Lowball Offers (Manejando Ofertas Bajas)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Rejecting or counter-offering an initial salary proposal that is significantly below market expectations.
**Engineering Impact**: Equips software engineers with the exact DELE-aligned Spanish phrasing to professionally discuss total compensation, highlighting C1/C2 executive engineering depth and LATAM vs Spain lexical differences.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **DELE Grammar Focus**: Conditional tense for polite requests (*Me gustaría*, *Estaría dispuesto a*), Subjunctive for hypotheticals (*Si me ofrecieran...*).
- **Tone**: Assertive yet collaborative. LATAM favors slightly more indirect politeness (*¿Sería posible...?*) compared to Spain's directness (*Me gustaría plantear...*).
- **C1/C2 Executive Depth**: Senior engineers must frame compensation as a reflection of business value, ROI, and scalable impact, rather than just personal desire.

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
**Context**: HR Recruiter (Reclutador) and Senior Backend Engineer (Candidato).
- **Reclutador**: Te hemos enviado la oferta oficial. ¿Qué te parece?
- **Candidato**: He revisado la oferta y, para ser transparente, la compensación total es menor a mis expectativas basadas en mi especialización en infraestructura. (*I have reviewed the offer and, to be transparent, the total compensation is lower than my expectations based on my specialization in infrastructure.* / *Tôi đã xem xét lời đề nghị và, thành thật mà nói, tổng mức đãi ngộ thấp hơn so với kỳ vọng của tôi dựa trên chuyên môn về hạ tầng.*)
- **Reclutador**: Es el máximo presupuesto que tenemos para este rol.
- **Candidato**: Lo entiendo perfectamente. Si el presupuesto es rígido, tendré que declinar la oferta por ahora, aunque me encantaría mantener la puerta abierta a futuro. (*I completely understand. If the budget is strict, I will have to decline the offer for now, although I'd love to keep the door open for the future.* / *Tôi hoàn toàn hiểu. Nếu ngân sách cố định, tôi sẽ phải từ chối đề nghị lúc này, mặc dù tôi rất muốn giữ liên lạc cho tương lai.*)

### 📚 Bilingual Technical Vocabulary
| Spanish (ES/LATAM) | English | Vietnamese |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Oferta a la Baja (Lowball) | Lowball Offer | Đề nghị giá quá thấp |
| Presupuesto Rígido / Tope Máximo | Strict Budget / Hard Cap | Ngân sách cố định / Mức trần |
| Declinar la Oferta | Decline the Offer | Từ chối lời đề nghị |

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake**: Using *Yo quiero* (I want) sounds demanding.
- **Correction**: Use *Me gustaría*, *Estaría buscando* or *Me interesa alinear* (I would like / I would be looking for / I am interested in aligning).
