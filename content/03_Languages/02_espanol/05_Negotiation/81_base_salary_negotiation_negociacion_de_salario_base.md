# 81. Base Salary Negotiation (Negociación de Salario Base)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Negotiating initial base salary for a software engineering role.
**Engineering Impact**: Equips software engineers with the exact DELE-aligned Spanish phrasing to professionally discuss total compensation, highlighting C1/C2 executive engineering depth and LATAM vs Spain lexical differences.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **DELE Grammar Focus**: Conditional tense for polite requests (*Me gustaría*, *Estaría dispuesto a*), Subjunctive for hypotheticals (*Si me ofrecieran...*).
- **Tone**: Assertive yet collaborative. LATAM favors slightly more indirect politeness (*¿Sería posible...?*) compared to Spain's directness (*Me gustaría plantear...*).
- **C1/C2 Executive Depth**: Senior engineers must frame compensation as a reflection of business value, ROI, and scalable impact, rather than just personal desire.

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
| Level | Spanish Phrasing (ES) | English (EN) | Vietnamese (VN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Junior (B1)** | Quiero más dinero. | I want more money. | Tôi muốn nhiều tiền hơn. |
| **Senior (C1/C2)** | Teniendo en cuenta mi experiencia en arquitecturas escalables, considero que un ajuste en la oferta reflejaría mejor mi valor en el mercado. | Given my experience in scalable architectures, I believe an adjustment to the offer would better reflect my market value. | Xét kinh nghiệm của tôi về kiến trúc mở rộng, tôi tin rằng việc điều chỉnh đề nghị sẽ phản ánh đúng hơn giá trị thị trường của tôi. |
| **LATAM Variant** | ¿Habría alguna posibilidad de revisar el sueldo base? | Would there be any possibility to review the base salary? | Có khả năng nào xem xét lại mức lương cơ bản không? |

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
**Context**: HR Recruiter (Reclutador) and Senior Backend Engineer (Candidato).
- **Reclutador**: Te hemos enviado la oferta oficial. ¿Qué te parece?
- **Candidato**: Agradezco mucho la propuesta. Sin embargo, basándome en el rango salarial actual del mercado para este nivel de seniority, ¿habría margen para negociar el salario base? (*I appreciate the proposal. However, based on the current market salary range for this seniority level, is there room to negotiate the base salary?* / *Tôi rất trân trọng lời đề nghị. Tuy nhiên, dựa trên mức lương thị trường hiện tại cho cấp bậc này, liệu có khoảng trống để đàm phán lương cơ bản không?*)
- **Reclutador**: El salario base está alineado con nuestro presupuesto, pero podemos revisarlo.
- **Candidato**: Excelente, un incremento del 10% me permitiría aceptar de inmediato. (*Excellent, a 10% increase would allow me to accept immediately.* / *Tuyệt vời, mức tăng 10% sẽ cho phép tôi chấp nhận ngay lập tức.*)

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake**: Using *Yo quiero* (I want) sounds demanding.
- **Correction**: Use *Me gustaría*, *Estaría buscando* or *Me interesa alinear* (I would like / I would be looking for / I am interested in aligning).
