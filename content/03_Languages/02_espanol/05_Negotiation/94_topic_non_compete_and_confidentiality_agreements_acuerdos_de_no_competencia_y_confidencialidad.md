# Topic: Non-Compete and Confidentiality Agreements (Acuerdos de No Competencia y Confidencialidad)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal:** Navigate Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Non-Compete clauses in Spanish with legal precision.
**Engineering Impact:** Ensuring you aren't legally locked out of future job opportunities in your specific tech stack or domain after leaving a company. Overly broad non-competes can severely stagnate a software engineer's career progression.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **Acuerdo de Confidencialidad (NDA):** Non-disclosure agreement.
- **Cláusula de no competencia:** Non-compete clause.
- **Propiedad intelectual (PI):** Intellectual property (IP).
- **Competencia desleal:** Unfair competition.
- **Período restrictivo:** Restrictive period.
- **Compensación económica:** Financial compensation (mandatory for non-competes in many jurisdictions).

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1):** "No voy a trabajar para la competencia." (I won't work for the competition.)
- **Senior (C1/C2):** "Me preocupa la amplitud de la cláusula de no competencia; considero que un período restrictivo de dos años a nivel global es excesivo para la dinámica actual del sector tecnológico. ¿Podríamos limitarlo a competidores directos y estipular la compensación económica correspondiente por ley?" (I'm concerned about the broadness of the non-compete clause; I consider a two-year global restrictive period to be excessive for the current dynamics of the tech sector. Could we limit it to direct competitors and stipulate the corresponding financial compensation per the law?)
- **LATAM vs Spain Callouts:** In Spain, post-contractual non-compete clauses are strictly regulated (Art. 21 Estatuto de los Trabajadores) and MUST include adequate financial compensation to be valid. In LATAM, enforcement varies widely: in Mexico, strict non-competes are often constitutionally unenforceable unless heavily compensated, whereas in Argentina they are viewed skeptically by labor courts unless strictly scoped to "competencia desleal".

**HR (EN/VN):** Es una medida habitual para proteger nuestra propiedad intelectual en algoritmos de IA.
*(EN: It's a usual measure to protect our intellectual property in AI algorithms.)*
*(VN: Đây là một biện pháp thông thường để bảo vệ sở hữu trí tuệ của chúng tôi về các thuật toán AI.)*

**Eng (EN/VN):** Lo entiendo. Sin embargo, restringe mi empleabilidad en cualquier empresa de software en toda España durante 12 meses sin contraprestación. ¿Estarían dispuestos a limitar esta restricción únicamente a competidores directos en el sector Fintech, o bien, a incluir una compensación económica durante el período de no competencia?
*(EN: I understand. However, it restricts my employability in any software company throughout Spain for 12 months without compensation. Would you be willing to limit this restriction only to direct competitors in the Fintech sector, or alternatively, to include financial compensation during the non-compete period?)*
*(VN: Tôi hiểu. Tuy nhiên, nó hạn chế khả năng tìm việc của tôi ở bất kỳ công ty phần mềm nào trên toàn Tây Ban Nha trong 12 tháng mà không có khoản đền bù. Các bạn có sẵn sàng giới hạn sự hạn chế này chỉ với các đối thủ cạnh tranh trực tiếp trong lĩnh vực Fintech, hoặc bổ sung một khoản bồi thường tài chính trong thời gian không cạnh tranh không?)*

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Native:** Assuming NDAs are strictly enforced without economic compensation in some jurisdictions (Spain often requires compensation for non-competes to be valid).
- **Non-native:** Saying "no competir" incorrectly as a noun (e.g., "la cláusula de no competir") instead of the correct legal term "la cláusula de no competencia".
