# Topic: Counter Offers and Leveraging Multiple Offers (Contraofertas y Negociación con Múltiples Ofertas)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal:** Professionally leverage competing job offers to negotiate higher compensation without burning bridges.
**Engineering Impact:** Maximizing your market value safely when in high demand. Tech is a small world; negotiating firmly but gracefully preserves your reputation while securing top-tier compensation.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **Contraoferta:** Counteroffer.
- **Oferta competidora:** Competing offer.
- **Igualar la oferta:** To match the offer.
- **Cerrar la brecha:** To bridge the gap.
- **Fecha límite / Plazo:** Deadline.
- **Sobre la mesa:** On the table (idiom used similarly in Spanish).
- **Proceso de selección:** Interview process.

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1):** "Otra empresa me paga más, ¿pueden pagar eso?" (Another company pays me more, can you pay that?)
- **Senior (C1/C2):** "Actualmente tengo sobre la mesa otra oferta muy competitiva. Sin embargo, su proyecto tecnológico y la cultura de ingeniería me resultan mucho más atractivos. ¿Habría flexibilidad para igualar la compensación base de esta oferta competidora para poder tomar una decisión inmediata y firmar con ustedes?" (I currently have another very competitive offer on the table. However, your tech project and engineering culture are much more attractive to me. Would there be flexibility to match the base compensation of this competing offer so I can make an immediate decision and sign with you?)
- **LATAM vs Spain Callouts:** "Cerrar la brecha" is understood everywhere, though "ajustar los números" is also common. In Spain, recruiters are usually direct about tight budget bands ("bandas salariales cerradas"); in LATAM, there might be more wiggle room depending on the urgency to hire. Bluffing is dangerous in both regions due to tight-knit tech hubs (like Barcelona, Madrid, or Guadalajara).

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue
*Context: A Backend Developer speaking with a Recruiter.*
**Eng (EN/VN):** Agradezco mucho la propuesta. Para ser totalmente transparente, he recibido una oferta de otra compañía que supera su paquete salarial en un 15%.
*(EN: I really appreciate the proposal. To be fully transparent, I have received an offer from another company that exceeds your salary package by 15%.)*
*(VN: Tôi rất trân trọng đề xuất này. Để hoàn toàn minh bạch, tôi đã nhận được một lời đề nghị từ công ty khác cao hơn gói lương của các bạn 15%.)*

**HR (EN/VN):** Entendemos. ¿Tu prioridad es estrictamente económica?
*(EN: We understand. Is your priority strictly financial?)*
*(VN: Chúng tôi hiểu. Ưu tiên của bạn có hoàn toàn là về tài chính không?)*

**Eng (EN/VN):** No exclusivamente. Valoro mucho la tecnología que utilizan y el rol propuesto aquí. Si pudieran acercarse a esa cifra o ajustar el paquete de unidades de acciones restringidas (RSUs) para cerrar la brecha, estaría encantado de firmar con ustedes hoy mismo.
*(EN: Not exclusively. I highly value the technology you use and the role proposed here. If you could get close to that figure or adjust the Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) package to bridge the gap, I would be delighted to sign with you today.)*
*(VN: Không hoàn toàn. Tôi rất đánh giá cao công nghệ các bạn sử dụng và vai trò được đề xuất ở đây. Nếu các bạn có thể tiến gần đến con số đó hoặc điều chỉnh gói Cổ phiếu thưởng hạn chế (RSUs) để thu hẹp khoảng cách, tôi sẽ rất vui mừng được ký hợp đồng với các bạn ngay hôm nay.)*

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Native:** Bluffing without a real offer can backfire quickly in tight-knit tech communities.
- **Non-native:** Sounding aggressive or like you are delivering an ultimatum ("Si no me pagan más, me voy a la otra empresa"). Failing to emphasize that you actually *prefer* the company you are negotiating with.

### Progressive Socratic Hints
1. **Hint 1:** Thank them for the offer and express strong interest.
2. **Hint 2:** Mention you are in the final stages (fases finales) of another process.
3. **Hint 3:** Request an extension to the deadline (solicitar una extensión de la fecha límite) to make an informed decision.

### 🗣️ Roleplay Short Answer
**Prompt:** Your current employer found out you are leaving and offered a counteroffer to stay. Decline it gracefully, stating you are looking for new technical challenges, not just money.

### 🏆 Model DELE C1/C2 Benchmarks
*Response:* "Agradezco sinceramente la contraoferta y el esfuerzo por retenerme; ha sido un honor trabajar aquí. No obstante, mi decisión de buscar nuevos horizontes no es estrictamente económica, sino que se basa en el deseo de enfrentar nuevos retos tecnológicos y cambiar de entorno arquitectónico, por lo que he decidido seguir adelante con la otra oportunidad."
