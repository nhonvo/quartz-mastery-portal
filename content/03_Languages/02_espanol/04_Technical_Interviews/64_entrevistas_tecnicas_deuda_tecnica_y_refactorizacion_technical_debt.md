# 64.Entrevistas Técnicas Deuda Técnica y Refactorización Technical Debt

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal:** Master articulating 64.Entrevistas Técnicas Deuda Técnica y Refactorización Technical Debt concepts in a Spanish technical interview with executive presence.
**Engineering Impact:** High-level articulation distinguishes mid-level engineers from Staff/Principal engineers.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory (C1/C2 Depth)
- **Executive Phrasing:** Focus on business impact, architectural trade-offs, and metrics. Use advanced structures like "A fin de que" (In order to), "Dado que" (Given that).
- **LATAM vs Spain Lexical Callouts:** 
  - *Spain:* Ordenador, fichero, fallo, entorno de pruebas, rendimiento.
  - *LATAM:* Computadora, archivo, error/falla, ambiente de QA, desempeño.

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1):** "Yo arreglé el problema y funcionó." (I fixed the problem and it worked.)
- **Senior (C1/C2):** "Lideré la resolución del incidente crítico, mitigando la deuda técnica y garantizando alta disponibilidad." (I led the critical incident resolution, mitigating technical debt and ensuring high availability.)

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue (LATAM/Spain Context)
**Entrevistador:** "¿Podrías detallar tu enfoque respecto a este tema de 64.Entrevistas Técnicas Deuda Técnica y Refactorización Technical Debt?" (Could you detail your approach regarding 64.Entrevistas Técnicas Deuda Técnica y Refactorización Technical Debt?)
**Candidato:** "Por supuesto. En mi rol anterior, identificamos un cuello de botella sistémico. Orquesté una refactorización que mejoró el throughput en un 40%." 
*(VN: "Tất nhiên. Trong vai trò trước đây của tôi, chúng tôi đã xác định được một nút thắt cổ chai hệ thống. Tôi đã điều phối một quá trình tái cấu trúc giúp cải thiện thông lượng lên 40%.")*

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake:** Literal translation of "I assumed" as "Asumí" (false friend, better: "Supuse").
- **Correction:** Use precise vocabulary suited for the region.
