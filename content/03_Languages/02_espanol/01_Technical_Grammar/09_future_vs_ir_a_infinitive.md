# 09. Future vs Ir a + Infinitive

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Distinguish between the Simple Future (desplegaré) and the Periphrastic Future (voy a desplegar) to communicate roadmaps, sprint goals, and long-term tech vision.

**Engineering Impact**: "We are going to deploy tomorrow" (concrete sprint task) vs "AI will replace this system in 5 years" (visionary/probabilistic). Using these tenses accurately delineates immediate actionable plans from strategic roadmap projections.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
- **Ir a + Infinitive (Periphrastic Future)**: Used for immediate plans, concrete sprint tasks, and things you are certain are about to happen. (Equivalent to "Going to").
  - *Example*: **Voy a revisar** el código en 10 minutos. (I am going to review the code in 10 mins).
- **Simple Future (Futuro Simple)**: Used for long-term roadmaps, distant plans, promises, or probabilistic assumptions about the present. (Equivalent to "Will").
  - *Example*: En 2026, **migraremos** toda la arquitectura a la nube. (In 2026, we will migrate all architecture to the cloud).
  - *Probability Example*: ¿Por qué falla? **Será** la caché. (Why is it failing? It's *probably* the cache).

**Bilingual Context (EN/ES/VN)**: 
*EN*: "I am going to fix it now vs I will fix it someday." 
*ES*: "**Voy a arreglarlo** ahora (Ir a) vs Lo **arreglaré** algún día (Future)."
*VN*: "Tôi **chuẩn bị/sắp** sửa nó bây giờ (Ir a) vs Tôi **sẽ** sửa nó vào một ngày nào đó (Future)."

### 🛠️ Real-World Engineering Context Comparison
- **Junior (B1)**: Yo miraré el ticket hoy. (Sounds like a vague promise rather than an actionable plan for today).
- **Senior (C1/C2)**: **Voy a investigar** el ticket ahora mismo. Eventualmente, **implementaremos** (simple future) una solución definitiva en Q3.
- **LATAM vs Spain Lexical Callout**:
  - *Spain*: ¿Cuándo **vais a lanzar** la beta? (When are you guys going to launch the beta?).
  - *LATAM*: ¿Cuándo **van a lanzar** la beta? / ¿Cuándo **saldrá** a producción?

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue (Roadmap vs Sprint)
**Product Manager (Lucía)**: Para el próximo año, **añadiremos** (simple future) soporte para machine learning. ¿Pero qué haremos en este sprint? (For next year, we will add support for ML. But what are we going to do this sprint? / Cho năm tới, chúng ta sẽ thêm hỗ trợ ML. Nhưng chúng ta sẽ làm gì trong sprint này?)

**Lead Dev (Jorge)**: En este sprint **vamos a optimizar** (ir a) las consultas SQL. Yo me **voy a encargar** de los índices. (This sprint we are going to optimize the SQL queries. I am going to handle the indexes. / Trong sprint này, chúng ta sẽ tối ưu hóa các truy vấn SQL. Tôi sẽ phụ trách các index.)

**Product Manager (Lucía)**: Me parece bien. Eso **mejorará** (simple future) mucho la retención de usuarios. (Sounds good. That will greatly improve user retention. / Nghe hay đấy. Điều đó sẽ cải thiện đáng kể khả năng giữ chân người dùng.)

### 📚 Bilingual Technical Vocabulary
| Español (ES) | English (EN) | Tiếng Việt (VN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Voy a desplegar | I am going to deploy | Tôi sắp/chuẩn bị triển khai |
| Desplegaré | I will deploy (eventually) | Tôi sẽ triển khai (tương lai xa) |
| El roadmap | The roadmap | Lộ trình (Roadmap) |
| A corto plazo | Short-term | Ngắn hạn |
| A largo plazo | Long-term | Dài hạn |

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- ❌ *Yo haceré el commit.* (Irregular future stems: hacer -> haré).
- ✅ *Yo haré el commit.*
- ❌ *Mañana yo compilaré el kernel.* (Too formal/distant for an immediate sprint task).
- ✅ *Mañana voy a compilar el kernel.* (Concrete short-term plan).

## Part 3: Assessment Exam

### ❓ MCQs
1. Which is best for a task you are starting right now?
   - [ ] A. Escribiré el script.
   - [ ] B. Voy a escribir el script.
   - [ ] C. Fui a escribir el script.
   *Answer: b*
2. Which form conveys a long-term architectural vision for 2030?
   - [ ] A. Vamos a usar microservicios.
   - [ ] B. Usaremos microservicios.
   - [ ] C. Usábamos microservicios.
   *Answer: b*
3. "I will do it later" (Irregular future of Hacer)
   - [ ] A. Lo haceré luego.
   - [ ] B. Lo haré luego.
   - [ ] C. Lo hacré luego.
   *Answer: b*
4. "We are going to deploy the hotfix today."
   - [ ] A. Vamos a desplegar...
   - [ ] B. Iremos a desplegar...
   - [ ] C. Desplegaremos... (acceptable, but less immediate)
   *Answer: a*
5. Using Future for probability: "Why is the server down?" -> "It's probably the memory."
   - [ ] A. Es la memoria.
   - [ ] B. Va a ser la memoria.
   - [ ] C. Será la memoria.
   *Answer: c*

### 🧪 Fill-in-the-Blanks
1. (Ir a) En este sprint, el equipo ________ ________ ________ (is going to refactor) el código legado.
2. (Future) En el futuro, la IA ________ (escribir) parte del código por nosotros.
3. (Ir a) Yo ________ ________ ________ (am going to check) los logs ahora mismo.
4. (Future) Si seguimos así, el proyecto ________ (tener) problemas de escalabilidad el año que viene.
5. (Future - Irregular) Nosotros ________ (poner) el sistema en cuarentena si hay una brecha.
*(Answers: 1. va a refactorizar, 2. escribirá, 3. voy a revisar, 4. tendrá, 5. pondremos)*

### 🔍 Error Refactoring
Rewrite these sentences applying the correct tense (immediate plan vs long term):
1. *Junior (for today's task)*: Yo resolveré este bug esta tarde.
   - *Refactored*: **Voy a resolver** este bug esta tarde.
2. *Junior (for 5 year vision)*: En 5 años vamos a estar en AWS.
   - *Refactored*: En 5 años, **migraremos / estaremos** completamente en AWS.
3. *Junior (for probability)*: Quizás es un error de DNS.
   - *Refactored*: **Será** un error de DNS. (Using future of probability).
4. *Junior*: ¿Qué tú hacerás mañana?
   - *Refactored*: ¿Qué **vas a hacer** mañana? / ¿Cuáles son tus tareas para mañana?
5. *Junior*: Yo teneré el reporte listo pronto.
   - *Refactored*: **Tendré** el reporte listo pronto (o **Voy a tener** el reporte listo en breve).
