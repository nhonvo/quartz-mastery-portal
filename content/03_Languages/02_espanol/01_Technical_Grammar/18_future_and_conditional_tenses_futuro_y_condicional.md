# 18. Future and Conditional Tenses (Futuro y Condicional)

## Part 1: Resource Lesson (Deep Dive Theory)

### 🎯 Executive Summary & Objectives
**Goal**: Master the Future (-é, -ás, -á) and Conditional (-ía, -ías, -ía) tenses to discuss roadmap planning, make technical estimations, and express polite hypothetical requests.

**Engineering Impact**: When planning roadmaps, differentiating between what *will* happen (Future) and what *would* happen under certain conditions (Conditional) is crucial for managing stakeholder expectations across cross-functional teams in Spain and LATAM.

### ⚙️ Contrastive Analysis & Theory
1. **Future (El Futuro)**: Used for actions that will happen, predictions, and estimations.
   - *Regular*: Infinitive + -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -án. (e.g., *desplegaré*, *desplegaremos*).
   - *Irregular stems*: tener->tendr-, hacer->har-, poder->podr-, decir->dir-.
   - *Example*: *El viernes **desplegaremos** la versión 2.0.* (On Friday we will deploy v2.0.)

2. **Conditional (El Condicional)**: Used for hypothetical situations ("would"), polite requests, and future in the past.
   - *Regular*: Infinitive + -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -ían. (e.g., *refactorizaría*).
   - *Irregular stems*: (Same as future) tendr-, har-, podr-, dir-.
   - *Example*: *Me **gustaría** usar GraphQL, pero no hay tiempo.* (I would like to use GraphQL, but there is no time.)

### 🎭 Role-Play Dialogue (Spain Tech Workplace)
**Context**: A sprint planning meeting discussing upcoming tech debt.

**Lucía (Product Manager)**: Javier, ¿**tendremos** (Future) lista la integración de Stripe para este viernes?
*(Javier, will we have the Stripe integration ready by this Friday?)*
*(Javier, chúng ta sẽ có sẵn bản tích hợp Stripe trước thứ Sáu này chứ?)*

**Javier (Tech Lead)**: Lo dudo. **Tendríamos** (Conditional) que pausar todo lo demás. Yo **sugeriría** (Conditional) moverlo al siguiente sprint. Así, **podremos** (Future) garantizar la seguridad del código.
*(I doubt it. We would have to pause everything else. I would suggest moving it to the next sprint. That way, we will be able to guarantee code security.)*
*(Tôi e là không. Chúng ta sẽ phải tạm dừng mọi thứ khác. Tôi đề nghị chuyển nó sang sprint tiếp theo. Như vậy, chúng ta sẽ có thể đảm bảo an ninh mã nguồn.)*

**Lucía**: Entendido. ¿**Podrías** (Conditional - Polite request) documentar eso en Jira, por favor?
*(Understood. Could you document that in Jira, please?)*
*(Đã hiểu. Bạn có thể document điều đó trong Jira được không?)*

### 🚫 Common Anti-Patterns
- **Mistake**: *Yo iré a revisar el código ahora.* (Using future for an immediate intended action).
- **Correction**: *Voy a revisar el código ahora.* (Use "ir a + inf" for immediate intentions. Use true Future for predictions/roadmaps).

2. **Hint 2**: "It would take" -> *Tomaría* or *Llevaría* (Conditional).
3. **Hint 3**: "We will include it" -> *Lo incluiremos* (Future of *incluir*).
