## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
What does '早上好' (zǎo shang hǎo) mean?
- [ ] A. Good evening
- [ ] B. Good morning
- [ ] C. Good night
- [ ] D. Hello

#### Question 2
How do you say 'Good night' in Mandarin?
- [ ] A. 晚上好 (wǎn shang hǎo)
- [ ] B. 晚安 (wǎn ān)
- [ ] C. 早上好 (zǎo shang hǎo)
- [ ] D. 下午好 (xià wǔ hǎo)

#### Question 3
If someone says '你好吗？', they are asking:
- [ ] A. Who are you?
- [ ] B. What is your name?
- [ ] C. How are you?
- [ ] D. Are you hungry?

#### Question 4
What is the correct response to '谢谢' (Thank you)?
- [ ] A. 不客气 (bú kè qi)
- [ ] B. 你好 (nǐ hǎo)
- [ ] C. 再见 (zài jiàn)
- [ ] D. 晚安 (wǎn ān)

#### Question 5
How do you say 'Good evening'?
- [ ] A. 早上好 (zǎo shang hǎo)
- [ ] B. 下午好 (xià wǔ hǎo)
- [ ] C. 晚上好 (wǎn shang hǎo)
- [ ] D. 晚安 (wǎn ān)


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: B** — It means Good morning.
2. **Correct Answer: B** — 晚安 means Good night.
3. **Correct Answer: C** — It means 'How are you?'.
4. **Correct Answer: A** — 不客气 means you're welcome.
5. **Correct Answer: C** — 晚上好 means Good evening.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ 早上安 (Good morning)
   - **Error**: Error: mixed up 'hǎo' with 'ān'.
   - ✅ 早上好 (zǎo shang hǎo)

2. ❌ 晚好 (Good night)
   - **Error**: Error: incomplete phrase.
   - ✅ 晚安 (wǎn ān)

3. ❌ 我好很 (I am very good)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong word order.
   - ✅ 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo)

4. ❌ 谢谢你吗？(Thank you?)
   - **Error**: Error: 'ma' is for yes/no questions.
   - ✅ 谢谢 (xiè xie)

5. ❌ 你好吗？(Meaning 'Hello')
   - **Error**: Error: 'nǐ hǎo ma' means 'How are you'.
   - ✅ 你好 (nǐ hǎo)
