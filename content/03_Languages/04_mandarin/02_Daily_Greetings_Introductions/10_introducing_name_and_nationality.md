## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
How do you ask 'What is your name?'
- [ ] A. 你叫什么？(nǐ jiào shén me?)
- [ ] B. 你是哪国人？(nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?)
- [ ] C. 你好吗？(nǐ hǎo ma?)
- [ ] D. 叫名字吗？(jiào míng zi ma?)

#### Question 2
What does '我是中国人' mean?
- [ ] A. I am an American.
- [ ] B. I am Chinese.
- [ ] C. I am a person.
- [ ] D. My name is China.

#### Question 3
To ask someone's nationality, use:
- [ ] A. 什么人 (shén me rén)
- [ ] B. 哪国人 (nǎ guó rén)
- [ ] C. 谁人 (shéi rén)
- [ ] D. 名字人 (míng zi rén)

#### Question 4
What is the Pinyin for '名字' (Name)?
- [ ] A. míng zi
- [ ] B. mǐng zī
- [ ] C. mìng zi
- [ ] D. míng zǐ

#### Question 5
What does the verb '叫' (jiào) mean in an introduction?
- [ ] A. To eat
- [ ] B. To look
- [ ] C. To be called
- [ ] D. To go


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: A** — 你叫什么名字 or 你叫什么 means what is your name.
2. **Correct Answer: B** — It translates to I am Chinese.
3. **Correct Answer: B** — 哪国人 means which country person (nationality).
4. **Correct Answer: A** — míng zi (2nd tone, neutral tone).
5. **Correct Answer: C** — 叫 means to be called or named.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ 我叫是王明 (My name is Wang Ming)
   - **Error**: Error: Do not use both 'jiào' and 'shì'.
   - ✅ 我叫王明 (wǒ jiào wáng míng)

2. ❌ 你什么名字？(What is your name?)
   - **Error**: Error: Missing the verb 'jiào'.
   - ✅ 你叫什么名字？(nǐ jiào shén me míng zi?)

3. ❌ 我是美国 (I am American)
   - **Error**: Error: Missing 'rén' for person.
   - ✅ 我是美国人 (wǒ shì měi guó rén)

4. ❌ 你哪国人？(What is your nationality?)
   - **Error**: Error: Missing the verb 'shì'.
   - ✅ 你是哪国人？(nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?)

5. ❌ 我名字叫是大卫 (My name is David)
   - **Error**: Error: Overly redundant grammar.
   - ✅ 我的名字叫大卫 / 我叫大卫
