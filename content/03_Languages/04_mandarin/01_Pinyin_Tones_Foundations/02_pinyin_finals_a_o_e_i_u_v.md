## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ nǔ (Female)
   - **Error**: Error: missing the umlaut (two dots).
   - ✅ nǚ (uses ü)

2. ❌ wǔ (I/Me)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong initial/final combination.
   - ✅ wǒ (uses o)

3. ❌ a (Hungry)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong final.
   - ✅ è (uses e)

4. ❌ yī (Five)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong meaning mapping.
   - ✅ wǔ (Five)

5. ❌ tā (It)
   - **Error**: Error: tā refers to He/Him usually in this context.
   - ✅ tā (He)
