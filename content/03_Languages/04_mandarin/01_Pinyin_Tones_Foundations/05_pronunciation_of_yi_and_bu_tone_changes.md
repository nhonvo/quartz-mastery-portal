## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
Before a 4th tone, 'bù' changes to:
- [ ] A. 1st tone
- [ ] B. 2nd tone (bú)
- [ ] C. 3rd tone
- [ ] D. stays 4th tone

#### Question 2
How is 'bù shì' pronounced?
- [ ] A. bù shì
- [ ] B. bū shì
- [ ] C. bú shì
- [ ] D. bǔ shì

#### Question 3
Before a 4th tone, 'yī' changes to:
- [ ] A. yì
- [ ] B. yí
- [ ] C. yǐ
- [ ] D. yi

#### Question 4
Before a 1st, 2nd, or 3rd tone, 'yī' changes to:
- [ ] A. yí
- [ ] B. yì
- [ ] C. yī
- [ ] D. yǐ

#### Question 5
How is '不吃' (not eat) pronounced?
- [ ] A. bú chī
- [ ] B. bù chī
- [ ] C. bǔ chī
- [ ] D. bu chī


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: B** — 'bù' changes to 'bú' before a 4th tone.
2. **Correct Answer: C** — bú shì (2nd + 4th).
3. **Correct Answer: B** — 'yī' becomes 'yí' before a 4th tone (e.g., yí gè).
4. **Correct Answer: B** — 'yī' becomes 'yì' before 1st/2nd/3rd tones.
5. **Correct Answer: B** — bù chī (stays 4th tone before 1st tone).
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ bù shì
   - **Error**: Error: bù does not change to 2nd tone.
   - ✅ bú shì

2. ❌ yī gè
   - **Error**: Error: yī does not change to 2nd tone.
   - ✅ yí gè

3. ❌ bú chī
   - **Error**: Error: changed to 2nd tone incorrectly.
   - ✅ bù chī

4. ❌ yí tiān
   - **Error**: Error: changed to 2nd tone instead of 4th.
   - ✅ yì tiān

5. ❌ bù duì
   - **Error**: Error: bù does not change to 2nd tone.
   - ✅ bú duì
