3. **Correct Answer: B** — '口' means mouth, used for family size.

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ 一个人 (yī gè kǒu)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong character for person.
   - ✅ 一个人 (yī gè rén)

2. ❌ 我家有三个人 (wǒ jiā yǒu sān gè rén)
   - **Error**: Error: generally 'kǒu' is preferred for family size.
   - ✅ 我家有三口人 (wǒ jiā yǒu sān kǒu rén)

3. ❌ rèn (Person)
   - **Error**: Error: wrong tone.
   - ✅ rén (2nd tone)

4. ❌ è (Two)
   - **Error**: Error: missing final consonant 'r'.
   - ✅ èr

5. ❌ 三口人 (sān kòu rén)
   - **Error**: Error: kǒu is 3rd tone.
   - ✅ sān kǒu rén
