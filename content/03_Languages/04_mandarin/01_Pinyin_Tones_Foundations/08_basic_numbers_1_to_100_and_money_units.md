## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
How do you say 'Fifty' in Mandarin?
- [ ] A. 五 (wǔ)
- [ ] B. 十五 (shí wǔ)
- [ ] C. 五十 (wǔ shí)
- [ ] D. 五百 (wǔ bǎi)

#### Question 2
What does '多少钱' (duō shǎo qián) mean?
- [ ] A. How are you?
- [ ] B. How much money?
- [ ] C. What time?
- [ ] D. How many people?

#### Question 3
What is the colloquial term for Chinese currency (Yuan)?
- [ ] A. 块 (kuài)
- [ ] B. 毛 (máo)
- [ ] C. 分 (fēn)
- [ ] D. 百 (bǎi)

#### Question 4
How do you say '100'?
- [ ] A. 十 (shí)
- [ ] B. 一百 (yì bǎi)
- [ ] C. 千 (qiān)
- [ ] D. 万 (wàn)

#### Question 5
If something is '十五块', how much is it?
- [ ] A. 15 Yuan
- [ ] B. 50 Yuan
- [ ] C. 10 Yuan
- [ ] D. 5 Yuan


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: C** — 五十 (wǔ shí) means 5 tens, or 50.
2. **Correct Answer: B** — It means how much money / how much does it cost.
3. **Correct Answer: A** — 块 (kuài) is the everyday term for Yuan.
4. **Correct Answer: B** — 一百 (yì bǎi) means one hundred.
5. **Correct Answer: A** — 十五 (shí wǔ) is 15.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ 五十块钱 (meaning 15 Yuan)
   - **Error**: Error: wǔ shí is 50, not 15.
   - ✅ 十五块 (shí wǔ kuài - 15 Yuan)

2. ❌ 二十百 (Two hundred)
   - **Error**: Error: 200 is 两百 or 二百.
   - ✅ 两百 (liǎng bǎi)

3. ❌ 多少块？(How much money?)
   - **Error**: Error: usually it's duō shǎo qián.
   - ✅ 多少钱？(duō shǎo qián)

4. ❌ 三十五毛 (35 Yuan)
   - **Error**: Error: máo is 1/10 Yuan.
   - ✅ 三十五块 (sān shí wǔ kuài)

5. ❌ 九十 (Nineteen)
   - **Error**: Error: jiǔ shí is 90.
   - ✅ 十九 (shí jiǔ)
