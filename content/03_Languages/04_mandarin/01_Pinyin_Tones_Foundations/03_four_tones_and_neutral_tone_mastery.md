## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
Which tone is high and flat?
- [ ] A. 1st Tone
- [ ] B. 2nd Tone
- [ ] C. 3rd Tone
- [ ] D. 4th Tone

#### Question 2
What does 'mǎ' (3rd tone) mean?
- [ ] A. Mother
- [ ] B. Hemp
- [ ] C. Horse
- [ ] D. Scold

#### Question 3
Which of these is a neutral tone?
- [ ] A. mā
- [ ] B. má
- [ ] C. mǎ
- [ ] D. ma

#### Question 4
Select the correct Pinyin for 'Scold':
- [ ] A. mā
- [ ] B. má
- [ ] C. mǎ
- [ ] D. mà

#### Question 5
The 2nd tone is described as:
- [ ] A. Rising
- [ ] B. Falling
- [ ] C. Falling-Rising
- [ ] D. Flat


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: A** — The 1st tone is high and flat.
2. **Correct Answer: C** — mǎ means horse.
3. **Correct Answer: D** — 'ma' without a mark is neutral.
4. **Correct Answer: D** — 'mà' (4th tone) means to scold.
5. **Correct Answer: A** — 2nd tone rises like a question.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ ma ma mà mǎ (Mother scolds horse)
   - **Error**: Error: First 'ma' needs tone 1.
   - ✅ mā ma mà mǎ

2. ❌ bù mā (Not numb)
   - **Error**: Error: Numb is tone 2.
   - ✅ bù má

3. ❌ má ma? (Horse?)
   - **Error**: Error: Horse is tone 3.
   - ✅ mǎ ma?

4. ❌ mà (Mother)
   - **Error**: Error: Mother is tone 1.
   - ✅ mā

5. ❌ mǎ (Scold)
   - **Error**: Error: Scold is tone 4.
   - ✅ mà
