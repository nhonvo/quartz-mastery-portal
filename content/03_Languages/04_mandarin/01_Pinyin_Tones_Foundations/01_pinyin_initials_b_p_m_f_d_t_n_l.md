- [ ] A. bā ba
- [ ] B. bà ba
- [ ] C. bá ba
- [ ] D. bǎ ba

#### Question 2
Which initial is in the word for 'Mom'?
- [ ] A. p
- [ ] B. d
- [ ] C. m
- [ ] D. f

#### Question 3
What does 'dà' mean?
- [ ] A. Small
- [ ] B. Big
- [ ] C. Take
- [ ] D. Mom

#### Question 4
Select the correct Pinyin for 'take':
- [ ] A. lā
- [ ] B. ná
- [ ] C. dā
- [ ] D. tǎ

#### Question 5
Which word uses the initial 'd'?
- [ ] A. 爸爸
- [ ] B. 妈妈
- [ ] C. 弟弟
- [ ] D. 拿


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: B** — bà ba is correct.
2. **Correct Answer: C** — mā ma starts with m.
3. **Correct Answer: B** — dà means big.
4. **Correct Answer: B** — ná means take.
5. **Correct Answer: C** — dì di starts with d.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ pa pa (Dad)
   - **Error**: Error: incorrect initial 'p'.
   - ✅ bà ba (uses 'b')

2. ❌ na na (Mom)
   - **Error**: Error: incorrect initial 'n'.
   - ✅ mā ma (uses 'm')

3. ❌ ta (Big)
   - **Error**: Error: incorrect initial 't'.
   - ✅ dà (uses 'd')

4. ❌ tí ti (Younger brother)
   - **Error**: Error: incorrect initial 't'.
   - ✅ dì di (uses 'd')

5. ❌ má (To take)
   - **Error**: Error: incorrect initial 'm'.
   - ✅ ná (uses 'n')
