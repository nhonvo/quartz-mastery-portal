## 🎯 1. Core Vocabulary & Pinyin Tones (Bilingual EN / VN)

* **[EN]**: Core daily vocabulary and Pinyin tone guide for **Module 07: Essential Measure Words (个, 张, 本)**.
* **[VN]**: Từ vựng và phiên âm Pinyin hàng ngày cho **Lượng từ cơ bản (个, 张, 本)**.

| Hanzi (汉字) | Pinyin Tone Mark (拼音) | English Meaning [EN] | Tiếng Việt Meaning [VN] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **个** | `gè (or ge)` | General measure word | Cái, chiếc (lượng từ chung) |
| **张** | `zhāng` | MW for flat objects (paper, table) | Tờ, chiếc (vật phẳng như giấy, bàn) |
| **本** | `běn` | MW for books | Quyển, cuốn (sách) |
| **书** | `shū` | Book | Sách |
| **桌子** | `zhuō zi` | Table | Cái bàn |

## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
Which measure word is used for books?
- [ ] A. 个 (gè)
- [ ] B. 张 (zhāng)
- [ ] C. 本 (běn)
- [ ] D. 口 (kǒu)

#### Question 2
Which measure word is best for a table (flat surface)?
- [ ] A. 张 (zhāng)
- [ ] B. 个 (gè)
- [ ] C. 本 (běn)
- [ ] D. 人 (rén)

#### Question 3
What does '个' represent?
- [ ] A. Book
- [ ] B. Table
- [ ] C. A general measure word
- [ ] D. Paper

#### Question 4
Select the correct phrase for 'one book':
- [ ] A. 一个书
- [ ] B. 一张书
- [ ] C. 一本书
- [ ] D. 一口书

#### Question 5
Select the correct phrase for 'two people':
- [ ] A. 两个人 (liǎng ge rén)
- [ ] B. 二个人 (èr ge rén)
- [ ] C. 两人 (liǎng rén)
- [ ] D. 两张人 (liǎng zhāng rén)


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: C** — 本 is used for books.
2. **Correct Answer: A** — 张 is for flat objects.
3. **Correct Answer: C** — 个 is the most common, general measure word.
4. **Correct Answer: C** — 一本书 uses the correct measure word.
5. **Correct Answer: A** — 两个人 uses liǎng + ge + noun.
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ 一个书
   - **Error**: Error: used the general measure word instead of 'běn'.
   - ✅ 一本书

2. ❌ 一张人
   - **Error**: Error: 'zhāng' is for flat objects.
   - ✅ 一个人

3. ❌ 二个人
   - **Error**: Error: used 'èr' instead of 'liǎng' with a measure word.
   - ✅ 两个人

4. ❌ 一本桌子
   - **Error**: Error: 'běn' is for books.
   - ✅ 一张桌子

5. ❌ 两个张纸
   - **Error**: Error: stacked measure words.
   - ✅ 两张纸
