## ❓ Section 1: Multiple-Choice Diagnostic Quiz (MCQs)

#### Question 1
When two 3rd tones appear together, the first one changes to:
- [ ] A. 1st tone
- [ ] B. 2nd tone
- [ ] C. 4th tone
- [ ] D. Neutral tone

#### Question 2
How is 'nǐ hǎo' actually pronounced?
- [ ] A. nì hǎo
- [ ] B. nī hǎo
- [ ] C. ní hǎo
- [ ] D. nǐ hǎo

#### Question 3
How should you read 'hěn hǎo'?
- [ ] A. hěn hāo
- [ ] B. hèn hǎo
- [ ] C. hén hǎo
- [ ] D. hěn hào

#### Question 4
What does 'kě yǐ' mean?
- [ ] A. Very good
- [ ] B. Watch
- [ ] C. Tiger
- [ ] D. Can/May

#### Question 5
How is 'shǒu biǎo' pronounced?
- [ ] A. shōu biǎo
- [ ] B. shóu biǎo
- [ ] C. shòu biǎo
- [ ] D. shǒu biáo


<details>
<summary>🔑 MCQ Answer Key & Explanations (EN / VN)</summary>

1. **Correct Answer: B** — The rule is 3+3 -> 2+3.
2. **Correct Answer: C** — It is pronounced as ní hǎo.
3. **Correct Answer: C** — hén hǎo due to sandhi.
4. **Correct Answer: D** — 'kě yǐ' means can/may.
5. **Correct Answer: B** — shóu biǎo (2nd + 3rd).
</details>

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or grammatical error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ nǐ hǎo (pronounced strictly as 3rd + 3rd)
   - **Error**: Error: Tone sandhi omitted.
   - ✅ ní hǎo (pronounce as 2nd + 3rd)

2. ❌ hěn hǎo (pronounced 3rd + 3rd)
   - **Error**: Error: Tone sandhi omitted.
   - ✅ hén hǎo

3. ❌ shǒu biǎo (pronounced 3rd + 3rd)
   - **Error**: Error: Tone sandhi omitted.
   - ✅ shóu biǎo

4. ❌ kě yí
   - **Error**: Error: The second syllable does not change to 2nd tone.
   - ✅ ké yǐ

5. ❌ lǎo hǔ (pronounced 3rd + 3rd)
   - **Error**: Error: Tone sandhi omitted.
   - ✅ láo hǔ
