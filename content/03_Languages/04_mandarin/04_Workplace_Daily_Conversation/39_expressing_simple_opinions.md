## 🎯 1. Core Vocabulary & Pinyin Tones (Bilingual EN / VN)

* **[EN]**: Core daily vocabulary and Pinyin tone guide for **Expressing Simple Opinions (我觉得这个很好)**.
* **[VN]**: Từ vựng và phiên âm Pinyin hàng ngày cho **Bày tỏ ý kiến đơn giản (我觉得这个很好)**.

| Hanzi (汉字) | Pinyin Tone Mark (拼音) | English Meaning [EN] | Tiếng Việt Meaning [VN] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **我觉得** | `wǒ jué de` | I think / I feel | Tôi cảm thấy / Tôi nghĩ |
| **这个很好** | `zhè ge hěn hǎo` | This is very good | Cái này rất tốt |
| **同意** | `tóng yì` | To agree | Đồng ý |
| **一般** | `yì bān` | So-so / Average | Bình thường |

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or Pinyin error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ wǒ jué dé (Wrong tone for de)
   - ✅ **wǒ jué de** (neutral tone in this context)

2. ❌ zhè ge hen hao (Missing tones)
   - ✅ **zhè ge hěn hǎo**

3. ❌ tōng yì (Wrong tone for tong)
   - ✅ **tóng yì**

4. ❌ yī bān (Wrong tone sandhi for yi)
   - ✅ **yì bān** (yi before 1st tone becomes 4th)

5. ❌ yǒu diān xiǎo (Wrong tone for dian)
   - ✅ **yǒu diǎn xiǎo**
