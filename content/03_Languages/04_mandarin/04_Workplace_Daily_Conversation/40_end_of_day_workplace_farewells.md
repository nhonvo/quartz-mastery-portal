## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or Pinyin error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ xīn kǔ lē (Wrong tone for le)
   - ✅ **xīn kǔ le** (neutral tone)

2. ❌ xià bān liao (Wrong pronunciation for le in this context)
   - ✅ **xià bān le**

3. ❌ míng tián jiàn (Wrong tone for tian)
   - ✅ **míng tiān jiàn**

4. ❌ lù shǎng (Wrong tone for shang)
   - ✅ **lù shang** (neutral tone)

5. ❌ da jia xin ku (Missing tone marks)
   - ✅ **dà jiā xīn kǔ**
