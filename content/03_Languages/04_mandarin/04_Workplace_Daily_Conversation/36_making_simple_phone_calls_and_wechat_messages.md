## 🎯 1. Core Vocabulary & Pinyin Tones (Bilingual EN / VN)

* **[EN]**: Core daily vocabulary and Pinyin tone guide for **Making Simple Phone Calls and WeChat Messages (喂, 我发微信给你)**.
* **[VN]**: Từ vựng và phiên âm Pinyin hàng ngày cho **Gọi điện & Nhắn tin WeChat (喂, 我发微信给你)**.

| Hanzi (汉字) | Pinyin Tone Mark (拼音) | English Meaning [EN] | Tiếng Việt Meaning [VN] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **喂** | `wéi` | Hello (on the phone) | A lô |

## 🔍 Section 3: Error Identification & Sentence Refactoring

Identify the tone or Pinyin error and rewrite it correctly:

1. ❌ wèi (Always 4th tone when answering phone)
   - ✅ **wéi** (Usually 2nd tone when answering the phone)

2. ❌ fā wěi xìn (Wrong tone for wei)
   - ✅ **fā wēi xìn**

3. ❌ sōu dào le (Wrong initial for shou)
   - ✅ **shōu dào le**

4. ❌ mǎ shàng kān (Wrong tone for kan)
   - ✅ **mǎ shàng kàn**

5. ❌ wǒ fa wei xin (Missing tone marks)
   - ✅ **wǒ fā wēi xìn**
